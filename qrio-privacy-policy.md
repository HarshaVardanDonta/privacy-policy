
# Privacy Policy for QRio

**Last updated:** 1 February 2026

QRio (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the **QRio – QR Code Generator & Scanner** mobile application (the “App”).

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Personal Information
QRio does **not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information** such as your name, phone number, address, or email address.

### b) Camera Access
The App requires access to your device’s **camera** solely for:
- Scanning QR codes

Camera data is processed **locally on your device** and is **never stored or transmitted** to our servers.

### c) Storage Access (if applicable)
Storage access may be used only to:
- Save generated QR codes to your device
- Read QR images selected by the user

We do **not** access or upload your personal files.

### d) Automatically Collected Data
QRio does **not collect analytics, tracking data, or device identifiers**.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

The App uses permissions only to:
- Generate QR codes
- Scan and display QR code content
- Save QR images locally (if chosen by the user)

No data is used for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

---

## 3. Data Sharing

We **do not share, sell, rent, or trade** any user data with third parties.

---

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user privacy:
- All QR processing happens locally on the device
- No data is uploaded to external servers

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

QRio does **not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13**.  
If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

---

## 6. Permissions Explanation

| Permission | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Camera | Scan QR codes |
| Storage (optional) | Save or read QR images |

Permissions are used **only when required** for core app functionality.
